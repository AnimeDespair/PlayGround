The method

Three passes. No noise.

Every session follows the same short ritual. Nothing is optimized for keeping you here.

01

Gather

Drop in the fragments. Links, doubts, a sentence you overheard. Nothing is too small to matter.

02

Constellate

Hermius looks for the hidden edges between things, then draws the smallest map that still holds.

03

Return

You leave with one question sharp enough to act on. The session ends. The door closes behind you.
